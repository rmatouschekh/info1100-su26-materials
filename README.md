## INFO 1100: Introduction to Programming (Summer 2026) Materials

The materials designed for the version of this class taught in summer 2026 at Cornell University by [Rebecca M. M. Hicke](https://rmatouschekh.github.io).

As an information science course, additional focused was placed on tools for data analysis applications. In response to the increased power and use of AI in and outside of the classroom, the course took two approaches. First, learning responsible and critical AI usage was scaffolded into assignments: students were asked to use AI in a variety of ways in the materials and reflect on their experiences and explain any generated code. Second, oral assessments were significantly integrated into the course – five oral quizzes were given and each assignment and the final project included oral assessments as part of the grade.

Please feel free to reach out to me if you are interested in rubrics or answer keys for these assignments, which have been excluded from the public repository.
